Northstar Labs — Fictional Employee Handbook

Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave each calendar year, excluding public holidays. Leave requests must be submitted through the People Portal at least ten working days in advance. A line manager approves requests. Up to five unused days can carry over into the next year and must be used by 31 March.

Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Team members agree their office days with their manager. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 UK time. Working abroad requires written approval from People Operations before travel.

Learning and development
Each employee has an annual learning budget of GBP 800. The budget covers approved courses, books and professional certifications. Employees request approval from their manager before purchasing. Unused learning budget does not carry forward.

Equipment and security
The company provides a laptop and a headset. Employees must enable multi-factor authentication on company accounts. Lost equipment must be reported to the IT helpdesk within two hours of discovery. Personal cloud storage must not be used for company documents.

Expenses
Expense claims must include an itemised receipt and be submitted within 30 days of purchase. Approved claims are reimbursed in the next monthly payroll. The meal allowance for approved business travel is GBP 35 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable.
